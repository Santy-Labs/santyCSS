Tap List

Iron Porter

Robust Porter · Dark Roast

7.2% ABV
38 IBU

Dark as molten iron. Notes of espresso, charred oak, and dark chocolate with a velvet finish that lingers like embers.

West Coast IPA · Citrus-Forward

6.8% ABV
72 IBU

Sharp as hammered copper. Aggressive citrus hops with a clean, bone-dry finish that cuts clean every time.

⚙️ The Process

How We Forge
Every Batch

01
🌾

Malt Selection

We source single-origin malted barley from family farms. Each varietal is roasted in-house to exact Lovibond targets.

02
🔥

Forge Mash

Temperature-controlled mashing at 0.5°C precision. Our custom copper lauter tuns extract maximum fermentable sugars.

03
🌿

Hop Strike

Wet-hopped with whole flower cones in season. Dry-hop additions at precise temperatures for maximum aroma without grassy notes.

04
🧪

Cold Ferment

Custom yeast strains propagated in-house. Lagers cold-conditioned for 6 weeks minimum at 34°F for crystalline clarity.

05
🪣

Barrel Aging

Limited series aged in bourbon, rye, and brandy barrels sourced from Kentucky and Scotland. Minimum 9 months per batch.

06
✅

Quality Forge

Every batch passes 22-point sensory analysis, forced fermentation tests, and a final blind panel before it sees a tap.
