— Our story

Building a home for your next chapter

Havenly was founded in 2017 on a simple belief — that finding a home should be joyful, not stressful. Today we're a team of 320 agents across 42 cities, powering 50,000+ happy moves.

— Our mission

Homes discovered with joy, moves made with confidence.

We combine deeply-local human expertise with thoughtful technology to make buying, selling and renting a home feel inspiring instead of exhausting. Whether it's your first studio or your dream villa, every move matters — and we treat it that way.

From transparent pricing and verified listings to concierge-level agent support, our job is to remove friction so yours can be focused on the life you're building.
