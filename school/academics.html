Academics

A rigorous, balanced curriculum from Grade 1 through Grade 12 — designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare.

🌱 Grade 1–5

Primary School

Our primary programme builds the essential foundations of reading, writing, numeracy, and scientific thinking — wrapped in a nurturing, activity-based environment where curiosity is celebrated.

📖 English🔢 Mathematics🌿 Environmental Science🎨 Arts & Crafts🎵 Music🏃 Physical Education🗣️ Hindi💻 Computer Basics🌍 Social Studies✏️ Creative Writing
🎯

Activity-Based Learning

Lessons are delivered through projects, games, storytelling, and hands-on activities for maximum engagement.

📊

Continuous Assessment

No high-stakes exams in Grade 1–3. Progress is tracked through portfolios, observations, and term projects.

👩‍🏫

Class Size: 28 Max

Small classes ensure every child receives individual attention from our certified primary educators.

📘 Grade 6–8

Middle School

The middle years deepen subject expertise, introduce project-based interdisciplinary learning, and build the critical-thinking and collaboration skills that define MERIDIAN graduates.

📐 Mathematics🔬 Science📖 English Literature🌍 Social Science🗣️ Hindi / French💻 Computer Science🎨 Visual Arts🏃 Physical Education🧪 Lab Work📊 Statistics Basics
🧪

Weekly Lab Sessions

Every Grade 6–8 class attends structured lab sessions in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology each week.

🌐

Project-Based Units

Semester-long interdisciplinary projects that connect Math, Science, and Social Studies into real problems.

🏆

Olympiad Coaching

Special preparation for NTSE, Math Olympiad, Science Olympiad, and English competitions.

🎓 Grade 9–12

Senior School

Our senior programme prepares students for CBSE board exams, competitive entrance tests, and higher education. Three streams — Science, Commerce, and Arts — with dedicated faculty for each subject.
