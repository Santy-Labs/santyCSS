📋 Process

How to Apply

A simple 5-step process from inquiry to enrollment. Our admissions team is available to assist at every stage.

1

Submit Online Application

Complete the online application form below with student details, grade applying for, previous school information, and contact details.

2

Document Verification

Upload or bring required documents (report cards, birth certificate, transfer certificate, address proof) to the admissions office within 5 working days.

3

Entrance Assessment

Students applying for Grade 2 and above appear for a written assessment (English, Math, Science) at the school campus. Grade 1 has an informal interaction session.

4

Offer Letter & Fee Payment

Selected students receive an offer letter within 3 working days. Admission is confirmed upon payment of the registration and first-term fee.

5

Welcome to MERIDIAN! 🎉

Attend orientation, receive your ID card and timetable, and join the MERIDIAN family. Our student mentors will help you settle in from day one.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an entrance test for Grade 1?
No. Grade 1 admissions are based on an informal interaction session between the student and the class teacher — no formal written test. We focus on readiness, communication, and curiosity.
Can we apply mid-year if seats are available?
Yes, mid-year admissions are considered on a case-by-case basis subject to seat availability. Please contact the admissions office directly for mid-year inquiries.
Are scholarships available?
Yes. MERIDIAN offers merit scholarships (up to 100% of tuition) and need-based financial assistance. Apply for scholarships during the application process — a separate scholarship form is included.
Is the school transport available?
Yes. We operate GPS-tracked, CCTV-equipped school buses covering 15+ routes across Chennai. Transport fee varies by distance zone. Contact the transport department for route details.
What documents are required for admission?
Required documents: (1) Last 2 years' report cards, (2) Transfer Certificate from previous school, (3) Birth Certificate, (4) Aadhaar Card (student), (5) Address proof, (6) 4 passport-size photos. For Grade 11–12 transfers: Mark sheets from Grade 9/10.
