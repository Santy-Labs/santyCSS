📅 Our Journey

A History of
Milestones

From a modest 200-student school to a 2,400-strong institution with national recognition — every year has been a chapter of growth.

1998
MERIDIAN Founded
Started with 12 teachers and 200 students in Grades 1–8 on a rented 3-acre campus in Chennai.
2002
CBSE Affiliation Granted
Received full CBSE affiliation and expanded to Grade 10 for the first time, with 400+ students.
2007
Grade 12 Introduced
Launched Science and Commerce streams for Grade 11–12. First batch cleared boards with 95% pass rate.
2015
New Campus Inaugurated
Moved to purpose-built 12-acre campus with modern labs, auditorium, and sports complex.
2020
A+ NAAC Accreditation
Received A+ NAAC accreditation and ranked #1 school in the district for the first time.
2026
27 Years Strong
2,400 students. 180 faculty. 48 national awards. Still growing, still committed.
